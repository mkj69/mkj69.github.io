Rethinking the Effectiveness of Contrastive Decoding in Mitigating Hallucinations in MLLMs

Original note, sentence-aligned / 原创笔记逐句对照。Not a full-paper translation / 不是论文全文翻译。

English / 中文 · Source / 原文

Separate the contrast from the candidate filter

将对比项与候选筛选分开

This study asks whether hallucination improvements attributed to contrastive decoding actually come from its contrastive term.

本研究追问：归因于对比解码的幻觉改善，是否真的来自对比项。

The authors analyze VCD, ICD, and SID, which construct amateur predictions through image corruption, misleading instructions, or internal visual-token selection.

作者分析 VCD、ICD 和 SID，它们分别通过图像扰动、误导指令或内部视觉词元选择构造业余预测。

These mechanisms should not be assumed to isolate only hallucination-related information.

不能预先假定这些机制只隔离出与幻觉有关的信息。

z_CD = z_E + α(z_E − z_A); V_head = {y : p_E(y) ≥ β max_w p_E(w)}

The contrast changes scores inside the surviving set, while the adaptive plausibility constraint changes which tokens can be sampled at all.

对比项改变保留集合内部的分数，自适应合理性约束则改变哪些词元有资格被采样。

A fair control therefore samples from the expert under the same filter without any amateur subtraction.

因此，一个公平对照是在相同筛选条件下直接从专家采样，不做任何业余模型减法。

The paper calls this control APC-S and also compares with ordinary sampling and greedy decoding.

论文将该对照称为 APC-S，并同时比较普通采样与贪心解码。

Discriminative diagnostics

判别任务上的诊断

For yes/no questions, the layer-wise margin is the Yes logit minus the No logit.

对于是否型问题，逐层 margin 定义为 Yes logit 减去 No logit。

m_l = z_l(Yes) − z_l(No); Δ_l = m_l^E − m_l^A

The analysis separates incorrect Yes answers from correct Yes answers before measuring whether the contrast selectively suppresses the former.

分析先区分错误的 Yes 答案与正确的 Yes 答案，再衡量对比项是否选择性压低前者。

AUC measures ranking selectivity, but the sign of the shift determines whether a particular Yes prediction is suppressed or amplified.

AUC 衡量排序选择性，而偏移的符号决定某个 Yes 预测被压低还是提高。

On LLaVA-v1.5-7B POPE-COCO, VCD's output-layer AUC is 0.523 with interval [0.49, 0.55].

在 LLaVA-v1.5-7B 的 POPE-COCO 实验中，VCD 输出层 AUC 为 0.523，区间为 [0.49, 0.55]。

Its mean shift on hallucinated Yes answers is +3.32 log-odds, and 96.5% of those answers are pushed further toward Yes.

对产生幻觉的 Yes 答案，其平均偏移为 +3.32 对数几率，且其中 96.5% 被进一步推向 Yes。

SID ranks better at AUC 0.691 but still has a positive mean shift of +1.61 on hallucinations.

SID 的排序 AUC 较高，为 0.691，但对幻觉的平均偏移仍为正的 +1.61。

Thus an above-chance ranking alone does not demonstrate corrective direction.

因此，高于随机的排序表现本身不能证明修正方向正确。

A tuned constant shift reaches 86.86% POPE accuracy versus 85.49% for greedy decoding in the reported control.

在所报告的对照中，调好的常数偏移达到 86.86% 的 POPE 准确率，贪心解码为 85.49%。

This supports a calibration explanation for that setting, rather than a universal impossibility result for contrastive methods.

这支持对该设置采用校准解释，而不是证明所有对比方法都不可能有效。

Free generation and causal attribution

自由生成与因果归因

As β increases, APC-S becomes more similar to greedy decoding because fewer candidate tokens survive.

随着 β 增大，APC-S 越来越接近贪心解码，因为留下的候选词元更少。

At β = 1, only maximum-probability tokens remain, with possible ties.

当 β = 1 时，只保留概率最大的词元，但可能存在并列。

The paper measures token-set Jaccard similarity and adds bigram and content-word controls because token sets ignore order.

论文衡量词元集合的 Jaccard 相似度，并增加二元词组和实义词对照，因为集合会忽略顺序。

A second control replaces the structured contrast with magnitude-matched random perturbations while keeping APC fixed.

第二个对照在保持 APC 不变的同时，用幅度匹配的随机扰动替换结构化对比信号。

Together these experiments ask which component deserves credit, rather than only whether a combined decoder beats plain sampling.

这些实验共同追问收益应归于哪个组件，而不只是组合解码器是否优于普通采样。

Interpretation and reproduction

解释与复现

The strongest design lesson is to hold temperature, support truncation, generated length, and judge protocol fixed when comparing guidance signals.

最重要的实验设计启示是：比较引导信号时，应固定温度、支持集截断、生成长度和评判协议。

Linear probes show that information is decodable, but do not by themselves establish that the generation circuit causally uses that information.

线性探针证明信息可被读出，但本身不能证明生成回路在因果上使用了这些信息。

Likewise, pooled candidate-level statistics can hide prompt-specific improvements and failures.

同样，汇总后的候选级统计可能掩盖提示词特定的改善与失败。

The paper should be read as a mechanism audit under its evaluated models and perturbations, not a blanket rejection of all log-ratio steering.

应将本文读作针对所评估模型和扰动的机制审查，而不是对所有对数比引导的一概否定。
