总览：reflow 为什么让一步生成更容易

三个主要结果

  • reflow 是蒸馏成功的关键（SD 1.4 初步实验，表 1）：直接蒸馏 SD 得到 FID-5k 40.9；先 reflow 再蒸馏得到 31.0，同时胜过 progressive distillation 一步的 37.2，训练只花约 24.65 A100 天。
  • 放大规模后达到 GAN 水平（SD 1.5，表 2）：InstaFlow-0.9B FID-5k 23.4、FID-30k 13.10（0.09 s）；InstaFlow-1.7B（Stacked U-Net）FID-5k 22.4、FID-30k 11.83（0.12 s）。
  • 2-Rectified Flow 本身就是好用的少步模型：步数≤4 时明显好于 SD 1.5 + DPM-Solver（图 9A），且一步模型的隐空间与它对齐，可兼容 SD 的 ControlNet。

一句话

InstaFlow 用纯监督学习把 Stable Diffusion 变成一步文生图模型：先做文本条件 reflow，把 SD 弯曲的概率流 ODE 拉直、改善“噪声 ↔ 图像”的配对（coupling），再把拉直后的 ODE 蒸馏成一次前向。没有对抗训练。InstaFlow-0.9B 生成一张 512×512 图只要 0.09 s，MS COCO 2014 上 FID-30k 13.1（StyleGAN-T：13.9，0.1 s）；MS COCO 2017 上 FID-5k 23.4（此前最好的一步 SD，progressive distillation：37.2）。训练总共 199 A100 GPU 天。

背景：大规模文生图一步生成还没人做成

  • SD 采样慢：SD 是隐空间里的 DDPM，通常要 100 步以上；DPM-Solver 等后处理采样器把它变成保边缘分布的概率流 ODE，可降到约 20 步，但少于 10 步时质量明显下降。
  • 蒸馏到一步失败：progressive distillation 能把 SD 压到 2 至 4 步，但一步 FID-5k 只有 37.2；作者自己直接蒸馏 SD 也“complete failure”（图 7）。
  • 现有一步大模型都是 GAN：StyleGAN-T、GigaGAN 靠对抗训练，生成器和判别器都要精细调参，训练成本 1792 / 4783 A100 天。

核心问题：直接蒸馏为什么失败？

  • SD 的概率流 ODE 轨迹是弯的，它定义的映射 X₀↦ODE[vSD](X₀) 对应一个“差”的噪声 ↔ 图像耦合：相近的噪声可能落到很不同的图，学生网络一步很难模仿。
  • 调超参救不了：9 组学习率 × weight decay 网格搜索，FID-5k 仍在 44 左右（附录表 3）。
  • 解法：先用 reflow 换一个更直、传输代价更低的耦合（边缘分布不变），再蒸馏。

只用最小二乘回归 + LPIPS：没有 GAN 损失，也不需要任何真实图像（训练数据只是文本提示词 + 教师模型生成的配对）。

记号、reflow、蒸馏和 CFG

记号（先看这个）

沿用 Liu et al. 的约定：X₀~π₀ 是噪声（SD 隐空间 4×64×64 的高斯），X₁~π₁ 是图像（的 latent），时间从 t=0（噪声）走到 t=1（图像）。

InstaFlow: Xₜ=(1−t)X₀+tX₁；SD3: zₜ=(1−t)x₀+tε

注：和 SD3 正好相反：本文 t=0 噪声、t=1 图像；SD3 里 t=0 是数据、t=1 是噪声，速度场方向也相反。读两篇时别混。

其余符号：𝒯 文本提示词；D𝒯 提示词数据集（laion2B-en 子集）；vₖ 第 k 个速度场；ODE[v](X₀|𝒯) 从 X₀ 解 ODE 到 t=1 的终点；𝔻(·,·) 图像相似度损失（LPIPS 或 L2）；α CFG 系数。

Rectified flow 回顾（Sec 2.2）

ODE 生成：学速度场 v，使

dZₜ/dt=v(Zₜ,t)，Z₀∼π₀ → Z₁∼π₁

训练目标（Eq 2）：γ 是 (π₀,π₁) 的任意耦合（最简单是独立配对 π₀×π₁），Xₜ 是任意可导插值：

minᵥ E(X₀,X₁)∼γ[∫₀¹ ‖dXₜ/dt−v(Xₜ,t)‖²dt]；Xₜ=ϕ(X₀,X₁,t)

DDIM / 概率流 ODE 对应 Xₜ=αₜX₀+βₜX₁ 的特殊选择（轨迹弯）；rectified flow 选直线插值（Eq 3）：

Xₜ=(1−t)X₀+tX₁ ⇒ dXₜ/dt=X₁−X₀

为什么要“直”：实际用 Euler 法求解（Eq 4），步长 1/N，t∈{0,…,N−1}/N：

Zₜ₊₁/N=Zₜ+(1/N)v(Zₜ,t)

若轨迹是匀速直线

Zₜ=tZ₁+(1−t)Z₀=Z₀+t v(Z₀,0)

则 N=1 一步 Euler 就是精确解（图 4）。所以“拉直轨迹”是降低推理成本的根本办法。

直线度：

S(Z)=∫₀¹ E[‖(Z₁−Z₀)−v(Zₜ,t)‖²]dt

S(Z) 越小越直，全部直线时为 0。

方法①：文本条件 reflow（Eq 5）

vₖ₊₁=argminᵥ ∫₀¹ Eₓ₀,𝒯 ‖(X₁−X₀)−v(Xₜ,t|𝒯)‖²dt
X₁=ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯)；Xₜ=tX₁+(1−t)X₀
ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯)=X₀+∫₀¹ vₖ(Xₜ,t|𝒯)dt
  • 一句话：用上一个流 vₖ 给每对（噪声，提示词）生成图像，得到配对 (X₀,X₁)，再用直线插值和 Eq 2 的损失训练 vₖ₊₁（多了文本条件 𝒯）。
  • 起点 v₁=vSD：直接把预训练 SD 的概率流 ODE 当作“1-flow”。所以 2-Rectified Flow 是第一次 reflow 的结果，不是 reflow 两次。
  • 离线配对：配对可以事先生成：ODE[vSD] 用 25 步 DPM-Solver、guidance scale 6.0（SD 1.4）或 5.0（SD 1.5，因为 6.0 会让 2-RF 过饱和）。每份数据 1,600,000 对。
# Algorithm 1（整理）：从 SD 训练文本条件 RF
v[1] = v_SD                    # 预训练 SD
for k in 1, 2, ...:            # 上限自定
    v[k+1] = copy(v[k])        # 从 v_k 初始化
    # 配对可离线生成
    pairs = [(x0, T, ODE[v[k]](x0|T))]
    for step:
        x0, T, x1 = sample(pairs)
        t = uniform(0, 1)
        xt = t*x1 + (1-t)*x0
        loss = |(x1-x0) - v[k+1](xt, t|T)|^2
        sgd(loss)
# v[k] 称为 k-Rectified Flow

reflow 的三条性质（来自原 RF 理论）：(1) ODE[vₖ₊₁] 与 ODE[vₖ] 的终点分布相同，vₖ 能生成正确分布则 vₖ₊₁ 也能；(2) 轨迹更直，需要的 Euler 步数更少；若 vₖ₊₁=vₖ（不动点）则轨迹严格是直线；(3) 对所有凸函数 c，传输代价不增：

E[c(ODE[vₖ₊₁](X₀|𝒯)−X₀)] ≤ E[c(ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯)−X₀)]

所以新耦合“可能更容易被学生网络学会”。实现：reflow 不改网络结构，只微调 SD 的 U-Net；学习率 10⁻⁶，AdamW，EMA 0.9999，梯度裁剪到 1，warm-up 1,000 步，BF16；文本编码器与 VAE 解码器冻结。

方法②：文本条件蒸馏（Eq 6）

为什么还要蒸馏：理论上要无穷次 reflow 才严格直；reflow 太多次又贵、又累积优化和统计误差。好在一两次 reflow 后就“接近直”，此时再蒸馏：

ṽₖ=argminᵥ Eₓ₀,𝒯 𝔻(ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯),X₀+v(X₀|𝒯))

学一个单步 Euler 映射 x↦x+v(x|𝒯)（网络时间固定为 t=0），用可导的图像相似度 𝔻 去逼近 X₀↦ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯)。论文的 𝔻 是 LPIPS（AlexNet 版，官方 0.1.4）；放大规模时先 L2 再换 LPIPS。

# Algorithm 2（整理）：蒸馏 k-RF 成一步模型
v_s = copy(v[k])
pairs = [(x0, T, ODE[v[k]](x0|T))]
for step:
    x0, T, x1 = sample(pairs)
    loss = D(x1, x0 + v_s(x0, t=0|T))
    sgd(loss)
# v_s 称为 k-Rectified Flow+Distill

不需要对抗训练，也不需要可逆网络（normalizing flow）。

reflow 与蒸馏是正交的

蒸馏：忠实复制已有映射 X₀↦ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯)，映射本身难学就学不好。

reflow：产生一个新的映射 ODE[vₖ₊₁]，因凸传输代价更低而更规则、更平滑。reflow 在形式上是蒸馏前的可选步骤，两者互不替代；但实践中作者发现蒸馏前必须先 reflow。

CFG 速度场（Eq 7）

vᵅ(Zₜ,t|𝒯)=αv(Zₜ,t|𝒯)+(1−α)v(Zₜ,t|NULL)

作用：仿照 classifier-free guidance，在文本条件 RF 上做外推。α 在多样性与质量之间权衡；α=1 时退回原速度场。论文对 2-RF 生成蒸馏数据时取 α=1.5。

注：两个 guidance 别混：SD 教师生成 reflow 数据时用 SD 自己的 guidance scale（6.0 / 5.0）；α 是 2-RF 采样时的系数。

与原始 RF 论文、SD3 的关系

  • vs Rectified Flow（Liu et al., ICLR 2023）：同一团队、同一套理论（直线插值、reflow、蒸馏）。原文只在 CIFAR-10 等小数据上验证（2-RF+Distill 一步 FID 4.85），1-RF 用直线插值从头训练；这里把 SD 的概率流 ODE 直接当 1-flow，新增文本条件、CFG 速度场 Eq 7、离线大规模配对和 Stacked U-Net。原文“一次 reflow 通常够用”在这里也成立：3-RF 结果有好有坏（表 4）。
  • vs SD3（Esser et al. 2024）：SD3 用 RF 目标从头训练大模型（MM-DiT），研究时间步采样与架构的规模化，仍是多步采样，不做 reflow 和蒸馏；InstaFlow 在已有 SD 上微调，目标是一步。一个解决“训练什么流”，一个解决“怎么把流压成一步”。记号相反（见第 2 页）。

最可能被问的三个问题

  • reflow 不改分布，凭什么帮蒸馏？它改的是配对：同样的边缘分布，换成更直、凸代价更低的噪声 ↔ 图像映射，学生更容易拟合。
  • 训练要真实图片吗？不要。D𝒯 只有提示词，X₁ 全由教师 ODE 生成。
  • 为什么 t 固定为 0？一步模型只在起点调用一次网络，所以蒸馏只用 t=0 这一项。

怎么读

InstaFlow-0.9B 与 PD 一步蒸馏成本相当（108 vs 108.8 A100 天），FID-5k 23.4 vs 37.2；0.1 s 档内 13.10 胜过 StyleGAN-T 的 13.90；但仍落后 GigaGAN（9.09，0.13 s）和多步扩散模型。

实验设置（Sec 3.1）

SD 1.4 初始化；batch 32，8 张 A100，AdamW。直接蒸馏：生成 32×100,000=3,200,000 对，训 100,000 步。为对齐成本，2-RF 训 50,000 步、再蒸馏 50,000 步；蒸馏数据 32×50,000=1,600,000 对，用 25 步 Euler、α=1.5 生成。

放大规模：训练流程与成本（Sec 4，附录 D）

  • InstaFlow-1.7B：从同一个 2-RF 出发，学生换成不共享参数的 Stacked U-Net（1.7B）。Distill 1：batch 64，110,000 步，L2，35.2 天；Distill 2：batch 320，2,500 步，LPIPS，4.4 天；共 39.6 A100 天（不含在 199 里）。
  • 成本换算：batch 4 的 U-Net，1 A100 天可跑 100,000 步（L2）或 86,000 步（LPIPS）。初步实验：16+4+4.65≈24.65 A100 天。对比：SD 1.4 从头训练 6250，StyleGAN-T 1792，GigaGAN 4783，PD 一步至少 108.8。

消融：分别删掉第 1 / 2 个 U-Net 的下采样块、上采样块，或中间的 In+Out 块；只有删中间 In+Out 不掉点，端到端时间 0.13 → 0.12 s（省 7.7%）。

  • 0.09 s 里有什么：文本编码 0.01 s + U-Net 0.04 s + 隐空间解码 0.04 s（不含 NSFW 检测）。
  • 为什么不用 2 步 PD：时间相近，但 2 步 PD 还要在 t=0.5 上拟合，对一步生成可能多余；把两个 U-Net 当一个模型还能剪掉冗余模块。

guidance 权衡：附录图 16，α=1.0 边缘模糊、细节扭曲；α≥2.0 逐渐过饱和；论文取 1.5。

应用（Sec 4，附录 E）

  • 快速预览：一步生成 512×512（约 0.1 s），插值到 1024 后交给 SDXL-Refiner 精修（图 10）。同样预算下可以多试很多种子再精修。
  • 隐空间对齐：同一噪声和提示词下，0.9B / 1.7B 一步模型与 25 步 2-RF 生成的图几乎一样（图 17），一步模型可当连续流的“代理”来分析隐空间。
  • 编码与重建：2-RF 反向解 ODE 把图编码成噪声，再正向解回来：
X₀=X₁+∫₁⁰ v(Xₜ,t)dt（encode）
X̂₁=X₀+∫₀¹ v(Xₜ,t)dt（decode）
  • 两步都用 Euler，4 步就能成功重建；因为 2-RF 更直，编码甚至 1 步就行（图 26）。
  • ControlNet：意外发现 InstaFlow 能直接用为 SD 预训练的 ControlNet（姿态、深度、边缘，图 27），说明一步模型基本保留了 SD 的特征空间。

局限（Sec 5）

复杂组合提示词仍是短板；作者认为更长训练、更大数据可缓解。附录 D：2-RF 没有完全收敛，蒸馏比 reflow 收敛快，换 LPIPS 立刻提升画质。质量上仍落后 GigaGAN 和多步 SD。

读完应该能回答的问题

1. 直接蒸馏 SD 为什么失败？弯曲 PF-ODE 给出的噪声 ↔ 图像映射不规则；网格搜索后 FID-5k 仍≥40。

  • 2. reflow 怎么帮蒸馏？分布不变但配对更直、凸代价更低；师生差距从 22.8 → 40.9 缩到 22.1 → 31.0。
  • 3. “2-RF”的 2 指什么？v₁=vSD，一次 reflow 得 v₂。
  • 4. 为什么只 reflow 一次？成本高、误差累积；3-RF 在 25 步与 Stacked 蒸馏上变差，且要把学习率降到 10⁻⁷。
  • 5. 199 天怎么来？16+11.2+64+54.4+53.6=199.2；1.7B 另加 39.6。
  • 6. 和 PD 比赢在哪？同等蒸馏成本，一步 23.4 vs 37.2；1.7B 用 0.12 s 得 22.4，优于 2 步 PD（0.13 s，26.0）。
  • 7. α 怎么选？增大 α：CLIP 升、FID 也升；α=1.5 折中。
  • 8. 需要真实图像吗？不需要，只需提示词集 D𝒯。
  • 9. 2-RF 一步不蒸馏行不行？不够：FID-5k 47.0（SD 1.5）/68.3（SD 1.4）；2 步 31.3。蒸馏仍是必要的最后一步。
  • 10. 为什么先 L2 后 LPIPS？附录 D 的流程如此；作者观察到换 LPIPS 能立刻提升画质。L2 阶段占蒸馏成本的一半左右（54.4/108）。
  • 11. 1.7B 为什么只花 39.6 天？直接从 0.9B 流程里训好的 2-RF 出发，只做蒸馏（论文没写是否另算数据生成）。
  • 12. 与 GAN 的本质区别？没有判别器和 minimax，只回归一个固定目标，训练稳定；代价是一步质量受老师（2-RF）上限约束。
  • 13. 图 6 说明了什么？reflow 后 S(Z) 显著下降，像素轨迹接近直线，这正是一步 Euler 可用的前提。

Overview: Why reflow makes one-step generation easier

Three main results

  • Reflow is central to successful distillation in the preliminary SD 1.4 experiments (Table 1). Direct SD distillation gives FID-5k 40.9. Reflow followed by distillation gives 31.0. This also improves on one-step progressive distillation at 37.2. Training costs about 24.65 A100 days.
  • At larger scale, performance reaches the GAN regime (SD 1.5, Table 2). InstaFlow-0.9B gives FID-5k 23.4 and FID-30k 13.10 at 0.09 s. InstaFlow-1.7B, with a Stacked U-Net, gives FID-5k 22.4 and FID-30k 11.83 at 0.12 s.
  • 2-Rectified Flow is itself a useful few-step model. With ≤4 steps, it clearly outperforms SD 1.5 + DPM-Solver (Figure 9A). Its latent space also aligns with the one-step model. This supports compatibility with SD ControlNet.

Central idea

InstaFlow turns Stable Diffusion into a one-step text-to-image model through supervised learning alone. Text-conditioned reflow first straightens SD's curved probability-flow ODE and improves the noise ↔ image coupling. Distillation then compresses the straighter ODE into one forward pass. Training has no adversarial component. InstaFlow-0.9B generates a 512×512 image in 0.09 s. Its MS COCO 2014 FID-30k is 13.1, compared with StyleGAN-T at 13.9 and 0.1 s. On MS COCO 2017, its FID-5k is 23.4. The earlier best one-step SD baseline, progressive distillation, gives 37.2. Total training costs 199 A100 GPU days.

Background: The missing large-scale one-step text-to-image model

  • SD sampling is slow. SD is a DDPM in latent space and typically needs more than 100 steps. Post-hoc samplers such as DPM-Solver express sampling through a probability-flow ODE that preserves the marginals. This reduces sampling to about 20 steps. Quality degrades noticeably below 10 steps.
  • Distillation to one step had failed. Progressive distillation compresses SD to 2 to 4 steps, but its one-step FID-5k is 37.2. The authors also describe their direct SD distillation as a “complete failure” (Figure 7).
  • The existing large one-step models in this comparison are GANs. StyleGAN-T and GigaGAN use adversarial training. Both generator and discriminator need careful tuning. Their training costs are 1792 / 4783 A100 days.

The central problem: Why direct distillation fails

  • SD's probability-flow ODE follows curved trajectories. Its map X₀↦ODE[vSD](X₀) gives a poor noise ↔ image coupling. Nearby noise inputs can map to very different images. A student network struggles to imitate this map in one step.
  • Hyperparameter tuning does not resolve the problem. A grid search over 9 learning-rate × weight-decay combinations still gives FID-5k around 44 (Appendix Table 3).
  • Reflow first replaces this coupling with a straighter one that has lower transport cost. The marginal distributions stay unchanged. Distillation follows this change.

Training uses only least-squares regression and LPIPS. It has no GAN loss and needs no real images. The training data consist of text prompts and pairs generated by the teacher model.

Notation, reflow, distillation, and CFG

Notation

The notation follows Liu et al. X₀~π₀ is noise, represented by a Gaussian in SD's 4×64×64 latent space. X₁~π₁ is the image latent. Time runs from t=0 at noise to t=1 at the image.

InstaFlow: Xₜ=(1−t)X₀+tX₁；SD3: zₜ=(1−t)x₀+tε

This convention is the reverse of SD3. Here, t=0 is noise and t=1 is the image. In SD3, t=0 is data and t=1 is noise. The velocity-field direction is also reversed. The two papers therefore use different endpoint conventions.

Other symbols: 𝒯 is the text prompt. D𝒯 is the prompt dataset, a subset of laion2B-en. vₖ is the kth velocity field. ODE[v](X₀|𝒯) is the endpoint obtained by solving the ODE from X₀ to t=1. 𝔻(·,·) is the image-similarity loss, either LPIPS or L2. α is the CFG coefficient.

Rectified flow background (Section 2.2)

ODE generation learns a velocity field v with the following dynamics:

dZₜ/dt=v(Zₜ,t)，Z₀∼π₀ → Z₁∼π₁

In the training objective (Equation 2), γ is any coupling of (π₀,π₁). The simplest choice pairs the endpoints independently through π₀×π₁. Xₜ is any differentiable interpolation:

minᵥ E(X₀,X₁)∼γ[∫₀¹ ‖dXₜ/dt−v(Xₜ,t)‖²dt]；Xₜ=ϕ(X₀,X₁,t)

DDIM and probability-flow ODEs correspond to particular choices of Xₜ=αₜX₀+βₜX₁, with curved trajectories. Rectified flow uses linear interpolation instead (Equation 3):

Xₜ=(1−t)X₀+tX₁ ⇒ dXₜ/dt=X₁−X₀

Straightness matters because sampling uses the Euler method (Equation 4). The step size is 1/N, with t∈{0,…,N−1}/N:

Zₜ₊₁/N=Zₜ+(1/N)v(Zₜ,t)

For a constant-speed straight trajectory,

Zₜ=tZ₁+(1−t)Z₀=Z₀+t v(Z₀,0)

a single Euler step with N=1 is exact (Figure 4). Straightening trajectories therefore provides a fundamental way to reduce inference cost.

Straightness measure:

S(Z)=∫₀¹ E[‖(Z₁−Z₀)−v(Zₜ,t)‖²]dt

A smaller S(Z) means straighter trajectories. S(Z) is 0 when all trajectories are straight.

Method 1: Text-conditioned reflow (Equation 5)

vₖ₊₁=argminᵥ ∫₀¹ Eₓ₀,𝒯 ‖(X₁−X₀)−v(Xₜ,t|𝒯)‖²dt
X₁=ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯)；Xₜ=tX₁+(1−t)X₀
ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯)=X₀+∫₀¹ vₖ(Xₜ,t|𝒯)dt
  • The previous flow vₖ generates an image for each noise-prompt pair. This gives paired endpoints (X₀,X₁). The next field vₖ₊₁ is trained on their linear interpolations with the loss in Equation 2 and the additional text condition 𝒯.
  • The starting point is v₁=vSD. The pretrained SD probability-flow ODE serves directly as the “1-flow”. Thus, 2-Rectified Flow is the result of the first reflow operation. It does not mean two reflow operations.
  • Pair generation can happen offline. ODE[vSD] uses 25-step DPM-Solver with guidance scale 6.0 for SD 1.4 or 5.0 for SD 1.5. A scale of 6.0 oversaturates 2-RF in the latter setting. Each dataset contains 1,600,000 pairs.
# Algorithm 1 (reorganized): Text-conditioned RF training from SD
v[1] = v_SD                    # Pretrained SD
for k in 1, 2, ...:            # A chosen iteration limit
    v[k+1] = copy(v[k])        # Initialization from v_k
    # Pairs may be generated offline
    pairs = [(x0, T, ODE[v[k]](x0|T))]
    for step:
        x0, T, x1 = sample(pairs)
        t = uniform(0, 1)
        xt = t*x1 + (1-t)*x0
        loss = |(x1-x0) - v[k+1](xt, t|T)|^2
        sgd(loss)
# v[k] is called k-Rectified Flow

The original RF theory gives three properties of reflow. (1) ODE[vₖ₊₁] and ODE[vₖ] have the same endpoint distribution. If vₖ generates the correct distribution, vₖ₊₁ does too. (2) Trajectories become straighter and require fewer Euler steps. At a fixed point, vₖ₊₁=vₖ, the trajectories are exactly straight. (3) Transport cost does not increase for any convex function c:

E[c(ODE[vₖ₊₁](X₀|𝒯)−X₀)] ≤ E[c(ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯)−X₀)]

The new coupling may therefore be easier for the student network to learn. Reflow keeps the architecture unchanged and only fine-tunes SD's U-Net. The settings are a learning rate of 10⁻⁶, AdamW, EMA 0.9999, gradient clipping at 1, a 1,000-step warm-up, and BF16. The text encoder and VAE decoder remain frozen.

Method 2: Text-conditioned distillation (Equation 6)

Distillation is still useful because exact straightness theoretically requires infinitely many reflow operations. Repeated reflow is expensive and accumulates optimization and statistical errors. After one or two reflow operations, the trajectories are already close to straight. Distillation then uses the following objective:

ṽₖ=argminᵥ Eₓ₀,𝒯 𝔻(ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯),X₀+v(X₀|𝒯))

The student learns the one-step Euler map x↦x+v(x|𝒯). Its network time is fixed at t=0. A differentiable image-similarity loss 𝔻 fits the teacher map X₀↦ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯). The paper uses LPIPS with AlexNet from the official 0.1.4 implementation. At larger scale, training starts with L2 and then switches to LPIPS.

# Algorithm 2 (reorganized): Distillation of k-RF into a one-step model
v_s = copy(v[k])
pairs = [(x0, T, ODE[v[k]](x0|T))]
for step:
    x0, T, x1 = sample(pairs)
    loss = D(x1, x0 + v_s(x0, t=0|T))
    sgd(loss)
# v_s is called k-Rectified Flow+Distill

Neither adversarial training nor an invertible normalizing-flow network is required.

Reflow and distillation are orthogonal

Distillation faithfully reproduces an existing map, X₀↦ODE[vₖ](X₀|𝒯). If that map is difficult to learn, the student approximation remains poor.

Reflow creates a new map, ODE[vₖ₊₁]. Its lower convex transport cost gives a more regular and smoother correspondence. Formally, reflow is optional before distillation. The two operations do not replace each other. In practice, the authors find that reflow is necessary before successful distillation.

The CFG velocity field (Equation 7)

vᵅ(Zₜ,t|𝒯)=αv(Zₜ,t|𝒯)+(1−α)v(Zₜ,t|NULL)

This field extrapolates text-conditioned RF in the style of classifier-free guidance. α controls the tradeoff between diversity and quality. At α=1, it reduces to the original velocity field. The paper uses α=1.5 when 2-RF generates distillation data.

The two guidance settings have different roles. The SD teacher uses its own guidance scale, 6.0 / 5.0, to generate reflow data. α is the coefficient used when sampling 2-RF.

Connections to the original RF paper and SD3

  • The original Rectified Flow paper (Liu et al., ICLR 2023) comes from the same team and provides the same theory of linear interpolation, reflow, and distillation. It tests small datasets such as CIFAR-10, where one-step 2-RF+Distill reaches FID 4.85. Its 1-RF is trained from scratch with linear interpolation. InstaFlow instead uses SD's probability-flow ODE directly as the 1-flow. It adds text conditioning, the CFG velocity field in Equation 7, large offline pairing datasets, and a Stacked U-Net. The earlier observation that one reflow operation is usually enough also holds here. The 3-RF results are mixed (Table 4).
  • SD3 (Esser et al., 2024) trains a large MM-DiT model from scratch with an RF objective. It studies timestep sampling and architectural scaling. Sampling still uses multiple steps, without reflow or distillation. InstaFlow fine-tunes an existing SD model for one-step generation. SD3 addresses the flow that is trained. InstaFlow addresses the compression of a flow into one step. Their notation uses opposite endpoint conventions (page 2).

Three likely discussion questions

  • Reflow helps distillation despite preserving the distribution. It changes the pairing. The same marginals admit a straighter noise ↔ image map with lower convex transport cost. The student can fit this map more easily.
  • Training needs no real images. D𝒯 contains only prompts, and the teacher ODE generates every X₁.
  • The network time is fixed at t=0. The one-step model calls the network only at the starting point. Distillation therefore uses only t=0.

Interpretation

InstaFlow-0.9B and one-step PD have comparable distillation costs, at 108 versus 108.8 A100 days. Their FID-5k values are 23.4 versus 37.2. Within the 0.1 s regime, InstaFlow's 13.10 improves on StyleGAN-T's 13.90. It still trails GigaGAN at 9.09 and 0.13 s, as well as multi-step diffusion models.

Experimental setup (Section 3.1)

The model starts from SD 1.4 and uses batch size 32, 8 A100 GPUs, and AdamW. Direct distillation generates 32×100,000=3,200,000 pairs and trains for 100,000 steps. For a matched training cost, 2-RF trains for 50,000 steps and its student for another 50,000 steps. The distillation dataset contains 32×50,000=1,600,000 pairs. It is generated with 25-step Euler sampling and α=1.5.

Few-step generation and guidance (Figure 9)

Guidance creates a tradeoff (Appendix Figure 16). At α=1.0, edges are blurry and details are distorted. At α≥2.0, oversaturation becomes increasingly visible. The paper uses 1.5.

Applications (Section 4, Appendix E)

  • Fast previews use one-step generation at 512×512, taking about 0.1 s. The result is interpolated to 1024 and refined with SDXL-Refiner (Figure 10). The same budget then allows many more seeds to be previewed before refinement.
  • The latent spaces align. For the same noise and prompt, the 0.9B / 1.7B one-step models produce nearly the same images as 25-step 2-RF (Figure 17). The one-step model can serve as a proxy for studying the continuous flow's latent space.
  • Encoding and reconstruction use 2-RF in both directions. The reverse ODE maps an image to noise. The forward ODE reconstructs the image:
X₀=X₁+∫₁⁰ v(Xₜ,t)dt（encode）
X̂₁=X₀+∫₀¹ v(Xₜ,t)dt（decode）
  • Both encoding and decoding use Euler sampling. Reconstruction succeeds with 4 steps. Because 2-RF is straighter, encoding can even use 1 step (Figure 26).
  • InstaFlow unexpectedly works directly with ControlNet models pretrained for SD. These include pose, depth, and edge conditioning (Figure 27). This suggests that the one-step model largely preserves SD's feature space.

Limitations (Section 5)

Complex compositional prompts remain a weakness. The authors suggest that longer training and more data could help. Appendix D notes that 2-RF has not fully converged. Distillation converges faster than reflow, and switching to LPIPS improves image quality immediately. Image quality still trails GigaGAN and multi-step SD.

Questions covered by the note

1. Failure of direct SD distillation. The curved PF-ODE gives an irregular noise ↔ image map. FID-5k remains ≥40 after the grid search.

  • 2. Reflow's contribution to distillation. The distribution stays unchanged, but the pairing becomes straighter and its convex cost decreases. The teacher-student FID change narrows from 22.8 → 40.9 to 22.1 → 31.0.
  • 3. The meaning of “2” in 2-RF. v₁=vSD, and one reflow operation produces v₂.
  • 4. The reason for using only one reflow operation. Repeated reflow is expensive and accumulates errors. The 3-RF results worsen for 25-step sampling and Stacked distillation. Its learning rate also has to decrease to 10⁻⁷.
  • 5. The source of the 199-day total. 16+11.2+64+54.4+53.6=199.2. The 1.7B model adds 39.6 days.
  • 6. The advantage over PD. At comparable distillation cost, one-step FID is 23.4 versus 37.2. The 1.7B model reaches 22.4 in 0.12 s. This improves on 2-step PD at 26.0 and 0.13 s.
  • 7. The choice of α. Larger α raises both CLIP and FID. α=1.5 provides the compromise.
  • 8. The need for real images. Training needs only the prompt dataset D𝒯.
  • 9. One-step 2-RF without distillation. Its FID-5k is 47.0 for SD 1.5 and 68.3 for SD 1.4. Two-step 2-RF gives 31.3. Distillation remains the necessary final step.
  • 10. The L2-to-LPIPS training sequence. Appendix D specifies this pipeline. The authors observe an immediate image-quality improvement after the switch to LPIPS. The L2 stage accounts for about half the distillation cost, at 54.4/108.
  • 11. The 1.7B model's 39.6-day cost. It starts directly from the 2-RF teacher already trained in the 0.9B pipeline and only adds distillation. The paper does not specify whether data-generation cost is counted separately.
  • 12. The difference from GAN training. There is no discriminator or minimax objective. Training regresses toward a fixed target and remains stable. One-step quality is nevertheless limited by the 2-RF teacher.
  • 13. The evidence in Figure 6. Reflow sharply reduces S(Z), and pixel trajectories approach straight lines. This is the condition that makes one-step Euler sampling useful.
